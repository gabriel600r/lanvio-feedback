## 1.0.0 — first public release

Your phone or tablet is the server, and you test from the browser of any device on your network, with nothing to install on the other end.

- **What it measures:** download, upload, ping, jitter, packet loss and bufferbloat graded A to F.
- **Always on:** the server keeps answering with the screen off, with a notification showing the test in progress and the last result.
- **History:** every test is saved with the device that ran it, with averages, a chart, filters and CSV export.
- **Test page** in English and Spanish, with its own browser icon.
- **Free with ads** on the Server and History screens, never on the test page. **Lanvio Pro**, a one-time purchase, removes them for good.
